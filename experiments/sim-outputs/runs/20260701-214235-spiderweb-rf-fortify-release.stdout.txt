Fortifying the RF lane with a fountain-code repair primitive

48 data chunks of 16B over the fading lane (same seed for both arms):

                       arm   raw delivered    after repair
--------------------------  --------------  --------------
     no repair (bare lane)         38/48 (21%)         37/48 (23%)
    + fountain (28 shards)         38/48 (21%)         48/48 ( 0%)

The bare lane drops ~21% and stays dropped. The fountain primitive reconstructs the
missing chunks from 28 repair shards (58% overhead) and delivers 48/48 — lossless.
So the RF lane can be USED without degrading delivery: it costs bandwidth, not data.
