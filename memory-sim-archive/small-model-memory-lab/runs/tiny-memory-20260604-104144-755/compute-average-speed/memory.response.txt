**Task:**  
A car travels 150 km at 60 km/h, then 90 km at 45 km/h. What is the average speed for the whole trip? Show distance, time, and final units.  

**Memory Patterns (retrieved):**  
- Average speed = total distance / total time.  
- Time = distance ÷ speed.  

**Calculation:**  
1. **First leg:** 150 km at 60 km/h → Time₁ = 150 ÷ 60 = 2.5 h.  
2. **Second leg:** 90 km at 45 km/h → Time₂ = 90 ÷ 45 = 2 h.  
3. **Total distance:** 150 + 90 = 240 km.  
4. **Total time:** 2.5 + 2 = 4.5 h.  
5. **Average speed:** 240 ÷ 4.5 ≈ 53.33 km/h.

- The result is exact (no rounding needed).
