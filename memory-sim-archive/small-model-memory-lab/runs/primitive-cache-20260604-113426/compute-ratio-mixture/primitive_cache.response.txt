- Setup: Let C = concentrate (L), W = water (L). Ratio is C : W = 2 : 5 → C/W = 2/5. Total volume is C + W = 42 L.
- Arithmetic: From C/W = 2/5 we get C = (2/5)W. Substitute into C+W=42: (2/5)W + W = 42 → (7/5)W = 42 → W = 30 L. Then C = 12 L.
- Units: Concentrate and water are measured in liters; the ratio is dimensionless, so the result is also in liters.
- Final answer: You need 12 liters of concentrate and 30 liters of water to make a total of 42 liters at a 2:5 concentration ratio.
