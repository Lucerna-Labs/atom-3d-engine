**Analysis:**  
The utility bill has two tiers: first 100 units at $0.12/unit, then any excess above 100 at $0.18/unit, plus a fixed fee of $6.  

For 135 units:  
- First 100 units: \(100 \times 0.12 = \$12\)  
- Excess over 100: \(135 - 100 = 35\) units at $0.18/unit → \(35 \times 0.18 = \$6.30\)  
- Fixed fee: $\$6$  

Total cost: \(12 + 6.30 + 6 = \$24.30\).

**Answer:**  
The bill is **$24.30**.
