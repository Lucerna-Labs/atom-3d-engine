**Task:** A cleaning mix uses concentrate and water in a 2:5 ratio. If you need 42 liters total, how many liters of concentrate and water are needed?  

**Answer:**  
- **Ratio parts:** 2 + 5 = 7 parts  
- **Concentrate per part:** 42 L ÷ 7 = 6 L  
- **Water per part:** 6 L × 5 = 30 L  
- **Final answer:** 6 liters concentrate, 30 liters water.
