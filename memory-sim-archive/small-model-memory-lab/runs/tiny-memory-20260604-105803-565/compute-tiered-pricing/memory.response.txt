**Analysis:**  
The utility bill has two tiers: $0.12 per unit for 0–100 units, then $0.18 per unit for any amount above 100 units. A fixed fee of $6 applies regardless of usage.  

For 135 units:  
- First 100 units at $0.12/unit = 100 × 0.12 = $12.00  
- Remaining (135 − 100) = 35 units at $0.18/unit = 35 × 0.18 = $6.30  
- Fixed fee = $6.00  

Total = $12.00 + $6.30 + $6.00 = **$24.30**

**Answer:** $24.30
