To solve this, we first determine the rate of one pump filling one tank. Since 1 pump fills 1 tank in 12 minutes, the rate is $1/12$ tank per minute.

For 5 pumps filling 10 tanks, the total work is $5 \times 10 = 50$ tank-minutes.
The time required is $50 \text{ tank-minutes} / (5 \text{ pumps} \times 1/12 \text{ tank/min}) = 50 \times 12 / 5 = 120$ minutes.

**Answer:** 120 minutes.
