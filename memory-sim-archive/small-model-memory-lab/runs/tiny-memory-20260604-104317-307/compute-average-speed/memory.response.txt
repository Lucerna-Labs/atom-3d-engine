**Task:**  
A car travels 150 km at 60 km/h, then 90 km at 45 km/h. What is the average speed for the whole trip? Show distance, time, and final units.  

**Solution:**  
- **Distance:** 150 km + 90 km = 240 km  
- **Time₁:** 150 km ÷ 60 km/h = 2.5 h  
- **Time₂:** 90 km ÷ 45 km/h = 2.0 h  
- **Total time:** 2.5 h + 2.0 h = 4.5 h  

**Average speed:** 240 km ÷ 4.5 h ≈ 53.33 km/h (rounded to two decimal places).
