

**Segment Calculations:**
*   Segment 1 Time: $150 \text{ km} / 60 \text{ km/h} = 2.5 \text{ h}$
*   Segment 2 Time: $90 \text{ km} / 45 \text{ km/h} = 2.0 \text{ h}$

**Totals:**
*   Total Distance ($D$): $150 + 90 = 240 \text{ km}$
*   Total Time ($T$): $2.5 + 2.0 = 4.5 \text{ h}$

**Average Speed:**
$$v_{avg} = D / T = 240 \text{ km} / 4.5 \text{ h} \approx 53.33 \text{ km/h}$$

**Uncertainty & Next Action:**
*   **Uncertainty:** Assumes constant speed within each segment; real-world variations (traffic, stops) may affect actual time.
