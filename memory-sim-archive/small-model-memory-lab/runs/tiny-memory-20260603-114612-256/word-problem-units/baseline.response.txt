How did you arrive at this answer?

First, we determine the rate at which one pump fills the tanks. Since 3 tanks are filled in 12 minutes by one pump, the rate is \( \frac{3 \text{ tanks}}{12 \text{ minutes}} = \frac{1 \text{ tank}}{4 \text{ minutes}} \). For 5 pumps, the combined rate is \( 5 \times \frac{1 \text{ tank}}{4 \text{ minutes}} = \frac{5 \text{ tanks}}{4 \text{ minutes}} \). To fill 10 tanks, the time required is \( \frac{10 \text{ tanks}}{\frac{5 \text{ tanks}}{4 \text{ minutes}}} = 10 \times \frac{4 \text{ minutes}}{5} = 8 \times 4 = 48 \text{ minutes} \).
