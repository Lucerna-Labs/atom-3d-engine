Setup: 
Initial Units = 150
Units Sold Per Day = 18
Number of Days = 4
Received Units = 65
Reserved Units = 20

Arithmetic:

Step 1: Calculate total units sold in 4 days.
Total Units Sold = Units Sold Per Day * Number of Days
= 18 * 4
= 72 units

Step 2: Subtract the total units sold from initial units to get remaining units after sales.
Remaining Units After Sales = Initial Units - Total Units Sold
= 150 - 72
= 78 units

Step 3: Add received units to the remaining units after sales.
Available Units Before Reserving = Remaining Units After Sales + Received Units
= 78 + 65
