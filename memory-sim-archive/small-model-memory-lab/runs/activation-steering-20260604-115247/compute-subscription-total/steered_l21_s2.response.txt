The total cost of the subscription over 6 months is calculated by adding the monthly cost to the number of months times the price per month, then subtracting the setup fee and adding the credit.

Monthly cost: $19.99
Number of months: 6
Price per month: $19.99

Total cost before adjustments = Monthly cost * Number of months
= $19.99 * 6
= $119.94

Setup fee: -$14.95 (credit)
Credit: +$10.00

Adjusted total cost = Total cost before adjustments - Setup fee + Credit
= $119.94 - $14.95 + $10.00
= $114.99
