**Setup:**  
Given: 4 printers → 480 pages in 6 minutes.  

Step 1: Find pages per minute for one printer.  
Pages per minute = \( \frac{480}{4} = 120 \) pages/minute.  

Step 2: Pages per minute for three printers.  
3 × 120 = 360 pages/minute.  

Step 3: Time needed for 720 pages at 360 pages/minute.  
Time = \( \frac{720}{360} = 2 \) minutes.  

**Answer:** 2 minutes
