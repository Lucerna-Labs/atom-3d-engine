**Task:**  
A car travels 150 km at 60 km/h, then 90 km at 45 km/h. What is the average speed for the whole trip? Show distance, time, and final units.  

**Answer:**  
- **Distance:** 240 km (150 + 90)  
- **Time:**  
  - First leg: 150 ÷ 60 = 2.5 h  
  - Second leg: 90 ÷ 45 = 2 h  
  - Total time: 4.5 h  
- **Average speed:** 240 km ÷ 4.5 h ≈ 53.3 km/h  

**Uncertainty/Next Action:** None required; calculation is straightforward and complete.
