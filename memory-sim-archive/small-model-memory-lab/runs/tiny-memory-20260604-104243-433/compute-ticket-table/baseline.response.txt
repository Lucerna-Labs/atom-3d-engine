Team A starts with 12 open tickets, closes 7, and receives 5 new tickets.  
Team B starts with 20 open tickets, closes 13, and receives 4 new tickets.  

**Calculation:**  
- Team A remaining: \(12 - 7 + 5 = 10\)  
- Team B remaining: \(20 - 13 + 4 = 11\)  

Total open tickets: \(10 + 11 = 21\).  

Team B has more open tickets.
