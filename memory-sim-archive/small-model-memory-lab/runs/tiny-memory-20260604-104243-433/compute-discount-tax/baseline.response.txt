**Step 1:** Calculate the discount amount.  
Discount = $80 × 25% = $80 × 0.25 = **$20**.  

**Step 2:** Find the discounted price.  
Discounted Price = Original Price − Discount = $80 − $20 = **$60**.  

**Step 3:** Apply sales tax to the discounted price.  
Sales Tax = $60 × 8% = $60 × 0.08 = **$4.80**.  

**Step 4:** Calculate the final price including tax.  
Final Price = Discounted Price + Sales Tax = $60 + $4.80 = **$64.80**.  

**Answer:** The final price is **$64.80**.
