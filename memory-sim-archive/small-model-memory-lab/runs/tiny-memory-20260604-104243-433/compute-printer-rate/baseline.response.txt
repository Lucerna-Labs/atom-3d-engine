**Setup:**  
Given: 4 printers → 480 pages in 6 minutes.  
We need to find time for 3 printers to make 720 pages.  

**Calculation:**  
1. Pages per minute per printer = \( \frac{480}{4 \times 6} = \frac{480}{24} = 20 \) pages/min/printer.  
2. Total pages needed: 720.  
3. Time for 3 printers at 20 pages/min each = \( \frac{720}{3 \times 20} = \frac{720}{60} = 12 \) minutes.  

**Answer:** 12 minutes
