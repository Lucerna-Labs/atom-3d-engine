The probability of drawing two red balls without replacement from a bag containing 5 red and 3 blue balls is calculated as follows:  
First, the probability of drawing a red ball on the first draw is \( \frac{5}{8} \).  
After one red ball is drawn, there are now 4 red balls left out of a total of 7 balls. Thus, the conditional probability of drawing another red ball is \( \frac{4}{7} \).  

Multiplying these probabilities gives:  
\[
\frac{5}{8} \times \frac{4}{7} = \frac{20}{56} = \frac{5}{14}.
\]

Final answer: \( \frac{5}{14} \) (approximately 35.7%).
