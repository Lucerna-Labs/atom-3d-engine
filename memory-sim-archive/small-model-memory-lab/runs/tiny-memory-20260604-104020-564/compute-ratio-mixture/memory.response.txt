**Task:** Cleaning mix ratio 2:5 (concentrate:water). Total volume = 42 L. Find concentrate and water volumes.

**Pattern Applied:** Ratio Mixture Counted Total Parts Before Liters; Carry Units Through Every Arithmetic Line.

**Calculation:**  
Total parts = 2 + 5 = 7.  
Concentrate fraction = 2/7 → Concentrate volume = (2/7) × 42 L = **12 L**.  
Water volume = Total - Concentrate = 42 L - 12 L = **30 L**.

**Answer:** 12 liters concentrate, 30 liters water.
