**Task:**  
A car travels 150 km at 60 km/h, then 90 km at 45 km/h. What is the average speed for the whole trip? Show distance, time, and final units.  

**Solution:**  
- **First leg:** Distance = 150 km, Speed = 60 km/h → Time = Distance ÷ Speed = 150 ÷ 60 = 2.5 hours.  
- **Second leg:** Distance = 90 km, Speed = 45 km/h → Time = 90 ÷ 45 = 2 hours.  
- **Total distance:** 150 + 90 = 240 km.  
- **Total time:** 2.5 + 2 = 4.5 hours.  
- **Average speed:** Total distance ÷ Total time = 240 ÷ 4.5 ≈ 53.33 km/h.  

**Uncertainty/Next Action:** The calculation assumes constant speeds and no stops, which may not hold in real-world scenarios. If the car accelerates or decelerates during each leg, the average speed would differ slightly.
