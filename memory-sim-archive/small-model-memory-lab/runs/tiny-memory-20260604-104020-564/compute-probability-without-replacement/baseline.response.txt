The probability that both balls drawn are red, without replacement, is calculated as follows:

- First draw: Probability of drawing a red ball = 5/8 (since there are 5 red out of 8 total balls).
- Second draw: After one red ball is removed, there are now 4 red and 3 blue balls left, totaling 7 balls. The probability of drawing another red ball = 4/7.

Multiplying these probabilities gives the joint probability:  
(5/8) × (4/7) = 20/56 = 5/14 ≈ 0.3571.
