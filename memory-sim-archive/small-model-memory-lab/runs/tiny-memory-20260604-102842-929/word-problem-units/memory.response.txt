**Setup:**  
Pump rate = 3 tanks / 12 min = 0.25 tank/min per pump.  
For 10 tanks, total volume needed = 10 × (rate of one pump) × time.  

**Calculation:**  
Time for 5 pumps to fill 10 tanks:  
(5 pumps) × (0.25 tank/min/pump) × t = 10 tanks → t = 10 / (5 × 0.25) = 8 minutes.

**Answer:**  
8 minutes.
