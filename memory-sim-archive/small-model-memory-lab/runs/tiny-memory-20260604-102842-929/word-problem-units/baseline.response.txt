**Setup:**  
Let \( P \) be the rate of one pump (tanks per minute).  
Given: 1 pump fills 3 tanks in 12 minutes → \( P = \frac{3}{12} = 0.25 \) tanks/min.  

For 5 pumps filling 10 tanks:  
Total work = \( 5 \times 10 = 50 \) tanks.  
Time required = \( \frac{\text{total work}}{P} = \frac{50}{0.25} = 200 \) minutes.  

**Answer:** 200 minutes.
