The task requires calculating a final price after two discounts and an additional tax. The recalled experience matches this scenario, so I will use it as the calculation method.

First, apply the 25% discount to the original cost of $80.  
Discount amount = 25% × $80 = 0.25 × 80 = $20.  
Discounted price = Original price - Discount amount = $80 - $20 = $60.

Next, apply the 8% sales tax to the discounted price of $60.  
Tax amount = 8% × $60 = 0.08 × 60 = $4.80.  
Final price = Discounted price + Tax amount = $60 + $4.80 = $64.80.

The final answer is $64.80.
