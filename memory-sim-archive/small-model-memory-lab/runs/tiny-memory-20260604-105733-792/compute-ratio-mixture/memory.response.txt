The task requires finding the quantities of concentrate and water for a 2:5 ratio with a total volume of 42 liters.  
First, determine the parts: 2 + 5 = 7 parts.  
Each part is 42 ÷ 7 = 6 liters.  
Concentrate: 2 × 6 = 12 liters.  
Water: 5 × 6 = 30 liters.  

Final answer: 12 liters of concentrate and 30 liters of water.
