

**Decision Frame: The "Reversibility & Cost" Matrix**

**1. The Core Trade-off**
*   **Option A (Fast/Risky/Reversible):** High opportunity cost of time, but low financial cost of failure.
*   **Option B (Slow/Safe/Expensive):** High financial cost of failure, but low opportunity cost of time.

**2. Decision Logic**
*   **Choose A if:** The time value of money is high, the risk of failure is low, or the project is a "learning experiment" where the cost of failure is negligible.
*   **Choose B if:** The financial cost of failure is catastrophic, the timeline is not critical, or the project requires high precision where speed introduces error.
