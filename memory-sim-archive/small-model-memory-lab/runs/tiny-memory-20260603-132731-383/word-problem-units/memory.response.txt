Let $P$ be pumps, $T$ be tanks, and $M$ be minutes. The rate is $\frac{3 \text{ tanks}}{12 \text{ min} \cdot 1 \text{ pump}} = 0.25 \frac{\text{tanks}}{\text{min} \cdot \text{pump}}$.

To find the time for 5 pumps to fill 10 tanks:
$$ M = \frac{10 \text{ tanks}}{5 \text{ pumps} \times 0.25 \frac{\text{tanks}}{\text{min} \cdot \text{pump}}} = \frac{10}{1.25} \text{ min} = 8 \text{ min} $$

**Answer:** 8 minutes.
