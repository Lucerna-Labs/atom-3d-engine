First, determine the rate of one pump:
$$ \text{Rate} = \frac{3 \text{ tanks}}{12 \text{ min}} = 0.25 \text{ tanks/min per pump} $$

Next, calculate the time for 5 pumps to fill 10 tanks:
$$ \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Total Tanks}}{\text{Number of Pumps} \times \text{Rate per Pump}} = \frac{10 \text{ tanks}}{5 \text{ pumps} \times 0.25 \text{ tanks/min/pump}} = \frac{10}{1.25} = 8 \text{ minutes} $$

**Answer:** 8 minutes.

*Uncertainty:* None; the problem assumes constant flow rates and no setup time.
