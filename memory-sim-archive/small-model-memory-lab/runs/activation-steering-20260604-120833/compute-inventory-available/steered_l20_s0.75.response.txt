Setup: 
Initial Units = 150
Units Sold in 4 Days = 18 * 4
Received Units = 65
Reserved Units = 20

Arithmetic:

1. Calculate the total number of units sold over 4 days.
   Units Sold in 4 Days = 18 * 4 = 72 units

2. Subtract the total units sold from the initial units to find out how many units remain after sales.
   Remaining Units After Sales = Initial Units - Units Sold in 4 Days
   Remaining Units After Sales = 150 - 72 = 78 units

3. Add the received units to the remaining units after sales.
   Total Units Available Before Reserving = Remaining Units After Sales + Received Units
   Total Units Available Before Reserving = 78 + 65 = 1
