1. Setup: 
   - Original Price (P) = $80
   - Discount Percentage (D%) = 25%
   - Sales Tax Rate (T%) = 8%

2. Calculate the discount amount:
   - Discount Amount = P * D% / 100
   - Discount Amount = $80 * 25 / 100
   - Discount Amount = $20

3. Subtract the discount from the original price to get the new price after discount:
   - New Price After Discount = P - Discount Amount
   - New Price After Discount = $80 - $20
   - New Price After Discount = $60
