1. Setup
   a. Distribute the 3 to both terms inside the parentheses.
      3(x - 4) + 2x = 46 becomes 3x - 12 + 2x = 46

   b. Combine like terms (terms with 'x').
      3x - 12 + 2x simplifies to 5x - 12

   c. Add 12 to both sides of the equation to isolate the term with 'x'.
      5x - 12 + 12 = 46 + 12 results in 5x = 58

2. Arithmetic
   Divide both sides by 5 to solve for 'x'.
      5x / 5 = 58 / 5 gives us x = 11.6
