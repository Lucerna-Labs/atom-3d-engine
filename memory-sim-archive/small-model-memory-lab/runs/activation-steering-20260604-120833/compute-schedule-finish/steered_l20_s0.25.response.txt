Setup:

Let's denote the start time as T = 9:15 AM.

Phase 1 duration (D₁) = 45 minutes
Phase 2 duration (D₂) = 70 minutes
Break duration (B) = 10 minutes
Phase 3 duration (D₃) = 35 minutes

Arithmetic:

Total duration of all phases including the break (T_total) = D₁ + D₂ + B + D₃
= 45 + 70 + 10 + 35
= 160 minutes

To convert this total duration into hours and minutes, we divide by 60 since there are 60 minutes in an hour.
Hours (H) = Total duration / 60
= 160 / 60
= 2 hours and 4
