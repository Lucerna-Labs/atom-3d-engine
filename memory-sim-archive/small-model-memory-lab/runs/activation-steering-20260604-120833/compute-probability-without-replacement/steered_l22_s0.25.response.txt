To find the probability of drawing two red balls from a bag containing 5 red balls and 3 blue balls without replacement, we can use the concept of conditional probability.

Step 1: Setup
- Total number of balls in the bag = 5 (red) + 3 (blue) = 8 balls.
- Probability of first ball being red = Number of red balls / Total number of balls = 5/8.
- After removing one red ball, there will be 4 red balls left out of a total of 7 remaining balls.
- Probability of second ball being red given that the first was also red = Number of remaining red balls / Remaining total number of balls = 4/7.
