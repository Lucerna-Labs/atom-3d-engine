To find the probability of drawing two red balls from a bag containing 5 red balls and 3 blue balls without replacement, we can use the concept of conditional probability.

Step 1: Set up the problem
- Total number of balls = 5 (red) + 3 (blue) = 8
- Probability of first ball being red = Number of red balls / Total number of balls = 5/8

Step 2: Calculate the probability after one draw
After drawing one red ball, there will be 4 red balls left out of a total of 7 remaining balls.
Probability of second ball being red given that the first was red = Remaining red balls / Remaining total balls = 4/7
