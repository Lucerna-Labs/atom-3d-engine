Setup: The cost of electricity can be calculated by adding the fixed charge to the product of the number of units used and their respective rates.

Arithmetic: 
- Cost for the first 100 units = 100 * $0.12/unit = $12
- Cost for the remaining (135 - 100) units = 35 * $0.18/unit = $6.30

Total cost before fixed charge = $12 + $6.30 = $18.30

Adding the fixed charge ($6), we get:

Final total cost = $18.30 + $6 = $24.30

Therefore, the bill for using 135 units would be $24.30.
