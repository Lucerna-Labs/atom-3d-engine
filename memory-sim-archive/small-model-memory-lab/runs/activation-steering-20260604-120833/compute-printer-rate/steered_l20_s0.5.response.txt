Step 1: Find the rate at which one printer prints.
We know that four printers make 480 pages in 6 minutes. To find out how long it would take one printer to make 480 pages, we divide the total time by the number of printers:

Time per printer = Total time / Number of printers
= 6 minutes / 4 printers
= 1.5 minutes per printer

So, one printer makes 480 pages in 1.5 minutes.
