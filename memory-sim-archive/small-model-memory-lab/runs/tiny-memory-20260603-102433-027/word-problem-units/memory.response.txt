 12 minutes

Setup: Let \( t \) be the time in minutes for one pump to fill one tank. Then, \( 3t = 12 \) minutes. Solving for \( t \), we get \( t = 4 \) minutes per tank. For 5 pumps, the time to fill one tank is \( \frac{4}{5} \) minutes. For 10 tanks, the time is \( 10 \times \frac{4}{5} = 8 \) minutes.
