**Setup:**  
Given: 4 printers → 480 pages in 6 minutes.  
Find: Time for 3 printers to make 720 pages.  

**Calculation:**  
Pages per minute = \( \frac{480}{4 \times 6} = \frac{480}{24} = 20 \) pages/minute.  
Time needed = \( \frac{720}{3 \times 20} = \frac{720}{60} = 12 \) minutes.  

**Answer:** 12 minutes
