task=probability
I remember a conditional probability failure where I used the whole population as the denominator after the prompt said "given." The correction was to step inside the given group first. If the question asks for A given B, the denominator is B, and the numerator is the overlap of A and B. After that change, the percent answer matched the condition instead of the population.
